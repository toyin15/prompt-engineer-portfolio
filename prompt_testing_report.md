## 🎯 Objective

To evaluate the effectiveness of Claude 3, GPT-4, and Gemini in generating engaging, structured YouTube scripts based on a creative content prompt.

---

## 🧾 Prompt Used

```text
You are a YouTube scriptwriter helping a Gen Z creator create a viral video.  
Generate a 2-minute script intro for the topic: “Why Gen Z Loves Silent Vlogging.”  
Make it casual, engaging, and TikTok-friendly. Avoid corporate tone.  
Include a compelling hook, 2–3 key points, and a short wrap-up or call to action.
```

## 🔁 Iteration Notes

**First Draft Observations:**
- Claude nailed tone and Gen Z familiarity
- GPT-4 was structured but leaned formal
- Gemini felt generic and missed TikTok-friendly tone

**Revised Prompt:**
> “Make it read like a TikTok voiceover script. Use Gen Z slang or emoji if appropriate.”

Claude adapted best, integrating terms like “lowkey” and “POV” for authenticity.

---

## 🧠 Findings

| Use Case                        | Best Model   |
|----------------------------------|--------------|
| Viral TikTok-Style Scripts       | Claude 3     |
| Structured Educational Content   | GPT-4        |
| Factual Summary / Metadata       | Gemini       |

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- Use Claude for social-driven, audience-aware copy
- Use GPT-4 for clean, structured scripts with light post-editing
- Avoid Gemini for creative-first content until updates improve nuance
